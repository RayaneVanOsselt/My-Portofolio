About

One point of contact,
from idea to screen.

I’m Rayane Van Osselt. I design web experiences end to end: I shape the user journeys, design the interfaces, then build them myself. What you approve in the mockup is exactly what goes live.

To me, a good website is felt before it is read: clear, fast and consistent down to the details. That’s what drives every decision I make, from the first wireframe to the last line of code.

My eye also comes from imagery: I am a sports photographer under the name rayvo.captures0808. Photo editing, video, illustration, layout: I take care of the visuals that bring an interface to life.

What I offer

    User journeys, wireframes, mockups and prototypes in Figma, designed to be understood in seconds.

  • 02

    Web development

    Custom, responsive and multilingual websites, or a tailored WordPress build when the project calls for it.

  • 03

    Visual content

    Photo editing, video, vector graphics and layout, for an identity that holds together on every medium.

Approach

A clear process,
a polished result.

Every project follows the same steps, so we move forward without surprises and approve each decision at the right time.

  1. 01

    Understand

    We talk through your goals, your audience and your content. I clarify what the website needs to achieve before designing anything.

  2. 02

    Design

    Site map, wireframes, then Figma mockups: you approve the design and user flows before the first line of code.

  3. 03

    Build

    I turn the mockup into clean code: responsive, accessible, fast, and multilingual if your audience needs it.

  4. 04

    Launch

    Testing on mobile, tablet and desktop, optimisation, then launch. And we fine-tune together if needed.
